## A case can lead to more than one conclusion

The official **26 July 2026** preview introduces **Curious Deductions**, a deduction mode with multiple endings. Your deductions affect the direction and conclusion of the investigation. This is a test-era introduction; the final launch flow still needs verification.

A useful way to read a case is to separate what you observed, what you inferred and which conclusion you reached. That is our note-taking suggestion, rather than a claim about required in-game steps.

## What Restart Deduction changes

The article says that, after uncovering an ending, **Restart Deduction** can revert **Evidence Board** progress so you can pursue another conclusion. It does not say this resets your whole account, all rewards or every unrelated quest.

The preview also says that revealing the case's truth unlocks a particular collectable and achievement. It does not list all endings or the exact rewards attached to every branch.

## An announced case: The Resurrection Mystery

The preview names **The Resurrection Mystery** and introduces a cemetery investigation and a scholar's death. We keep this introduction brief to preserve the mystery. The official article contains further premise details and screenshots.

Its Japanese title is **死者蘇生の謎**. The mode is **奇事件の再演**, the board is **捜査ボード**, and the restart action is **再推理**. These localized terms can help when following a video or discussing a case across languages.
