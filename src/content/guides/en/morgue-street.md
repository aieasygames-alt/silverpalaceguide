## A crossroads within High Peers

Elementa's **14 July 2026** local preview describes **Morgue Street** as an important enclave within **High Peers**, connecting several districts and bringing together people from different places and social backgrounds. Its Japanese names are **モーグ街区** and **ハイ・ピアーズ地区**.

The article labels the content a work in progress. This page is a setting reference: it does not establish final map boundaries, travel times or collectible coverage.

The launch announcement places the adventure in Silvernia; the local preview places Morgue Street within High Peers. Read the names at those different levels. A district name and an area name do not describe two separate destinations of equal scope, and this hierarchy is not a route or a measured map.

## Three kinds of places to recognize

The official preview introduces several landmark functions:

- **The Detective's agency:** a combined workplace and modest home, used as a base for investigations.
- **A busy tavern:** a nighttime gathering place for many kinds of people. The preview connects its conversations and rumors with possible clues.
- **A lively café:** a meeting place for intellectuals, clerks and merchants discussing city affairs between shifts.

These descriptions help you recognize the character of the area. They do not establish a specific quest trigger, opening schedule or a guaranteed item reward. Some landmark names are part of the article's images; we keep the text here at the level verified in both localized descriptions.

## Who is connected to the Detective’s agency?

The area preview describes the agency as the Detective’s workplace and home. The separate official character introduction names **Cynthia II (Luna’s Scion)** as the Detective’s landlady; the Japanese introduction specifically refers to the agency. This connects a landmark’s function with a named person in the cast.

Read [Cynthia II’s introduction](../../characters/#cynthia-ii) alongside the [Detective’s shared biography](../../characters/#detective) for that background. The tavern description, by contrast, does not explicitly identify its bartender. Argos’s occupation alone is not enough to assign him to this particular building.

## Rival powers and everyday danger

The preview presents Morgue Street as a place where no single faction has secured complete control. That balance creates both danger and opportunity.

It describes **Metal-Blooded** creatures that can pass for ordinary citizens until exposed to high-purity **Silverium**. It also introduces a loose coalition of gangs and the petty thugs found in the city's streets and alleys. These are qualitative background descriptions, not verified enemy weaknesses, spawn points or farming information.

## Stonefall Syndicate: the English and Japanese names

The English article’s [gang illustration](https://s.elementagames.com/official/image/20260709/bd7b8c91e0afaaf1316a23569c7fb263.jpg) is headed **Stonefall Syndicate**. The corresponding Japanese caption names **ロアリングストーンズ**. Matching the illustrations and their descriptions matters here: a literal English translation of the Japanese name would miss the localized English heading.

The caption describes a shared banner used by several smaller gangs, with a loose internal structure and a threat arising in part from their numbers. It does not present a single uniform organization with a confirmed combat roster. The petty thugs shown separately are not explicitly assigned to that coalition by the caption.

**Metal-Blooded** are described differently, through their disguise and Silverium-related transformation. This is background about what they are, rather than evidence that a Silverium item is an effective attack or that a particular damage type is their weakness. Recognizing the distinction keeps the setting information separate from an enemy strategy.
