New to **Silver Palace**? Start with the question you need answered now, then follow the relevant reference. The order below is our editorial suggestion. It is based on official announcements and labeled beta previews, rather than a tested opening-hour walkthrough.

If you want to understand the setting and the game’s overall structure first, read [what Silver Palace is about](../game-overview/): it connects the Detective’s story, city exploration, investigations and combat.

## Before launch: settle the practical questions

Elementa announced **14 December 2026 (UTC+8)** as the global launch date, on PC, PlayStation 5, iOS and Android. The announcement does not give an opening hour. Check the [launch brief](../launch-preparation/) for the date and event deadlines, then open the [official platform links](../platforms-downloads/) for your region and device.

Before downloading or linking accounts, confirm three things on current official pages: device compatibility, the opening time for your region, and the account rules for any platforms you plan to use. Neither a PC listing nor the announcement's platform list establishes cross-save or purchase-transfer rules.

If you joined **Mystery Unveiled**, record what you actually drew in the [event reward worksheet](../../tools/event-rewards/). The official rules allow up to six drawn **entries** to be claimed; identical item names can occupy separate entries. Drawing, planning a selection and claiming are different actions. Claiming must be completed on the official event site after creating the receiving game character. Read the [full event rules summary](../mystery-unveiled/) before making that one-time choice.

## Your first fight: look for two different openings

The official July beta combat tutorial describes two useful situations to recognize:

- **An enemy is preparing an attack:** a yellow circle is the announced cue. A well-timed **Parry** reduces incoming damage and counters; successful parries can lead into follow-up attacks.
- **An enemy is already Stunned:** an **Execution** deals substantial damage and is followed by a temporary **Weakened State**, creating another attack opportunity.

Do not assume those are one guaranteed chain. The source does not say every parry stuns an enemy, or that every attack can be parried. When the game becomes available to you, use its current tutorial for timing and your platform's controls. The [combat reference](../combat-basics/) separates the two situations and explains which details remain unverified for launch.

## Switching characters: learn the action before a rotation

The same beta article describes **Synchronised Combat**: switch during a Charged Attack or Charged Skill, and the outgoing character can finish that action before leaving the field. It also describes attacks building **Resonance**, with an effect linked to the character's Reactor attribute.

These are mechanisms to recognize when reading the tutorial. They do not establish an optimal rotation, damage formula or universally compatible team. The article's Casual Mode / シンプルモード description is another setting to check against the current game; its left-click example does not specify final controller or touch bindings.

Use the [official character dossiers](../../characters/) to learn names, backgrounds and announced equipment. The [comparison tool](../../tools/character-compare/) puts up to three introductions side by side. Occupations and equipment descriptions alone are not enough to choose a strongest character or spend scarce resources on a claimed “best team.”

## Your first investigation: separate an observation from a guess

The official deduction preview describes **Curious Deductions** with multiple endings. After reaching an ending, **Restart Deduction** can revert **Evidence Board** progress to explore another conclusion. The preview does not say it resets your entire account or every unrelated reward.

For your own notes, use three short lines:

- **Observed:** the exact clue, statement or object you saw.
- **Possible explanation:** your current interpretation, kept separate from the observation.
- **Result:** the conclusion or ending you actually reached.

This is a suggested note-taking method, not a required in-game sequence. Keep case-sensitive notes in your [personal notebook](../../exploration/) and export a backup if you need to keep them. Choose your preferred level of help with the [three-level hint explanation](../reading-case-hints/); a full answer should be a deliberate choice. The [deduction reference](../curious-deductions/) explains the official mechanism without revealing a case solution.

## Exploring the city: leave a useful return note

The official **Morgue Street** article places the district within **High Peers** and describes its landmarks. It is a development-period introduction, not a verified route or collectible map. Read the [region reference](../morgue-street/) for that context.

When you find something you want to revisit, a useful personal note records a recognizable landmark, your direction of approach, the interaction you tried and what stopped you. Add the current task or ability requirement only if the game actually shows it. A note such as “return when the displayed condition is met” preserves the question without inventing an unlock rule.

Our [exploration checklist](../../exploration/) can hold those tasks and your completion marks. Notes stay in this browser; switching between this site's English and Japanese pages keeps the same list, while another device needs an exported backup.

## Planning an upgrade: record the whole cost

When you can inspect an upgrade in the game, record the character, upgrade type, starting and target stage, every material and any currency cost. Then enter confirmed required and owned quantities in the [material planner](../../tools/material-planner/). It calculates **shortage = max(required − owned, 0)**.

An unknown quantity is different from a confirmed zero. Leave an uncertain upgrade out of a completed plan until its full cost is visible; web-event reward amounts do not establish upgrade costs or drop rates. The planner currently supports your own entries. Verified character presets require complete interval records and an independent check.

Keep a backup of your worksheet before changing devices or clearing browser data. Read [how material planning works](../planning-materials/) for the calculation and save behavior.

## Playing across languages: look up the official term

If a video, conversation or menu uses another language, search the [English–Japanese reference](../../tools/glossary/) for the official name. For example, Parries correspond to **パリィ**, Curious Deductions to **奇事件の再演**, and the Evidence Board to **捜査ボード**.

A matching name helps you identify the same mechanism; it does not show that a July test and the launch build behave identically. Check the date and scope on the linked guide before following an instruction from another version.
