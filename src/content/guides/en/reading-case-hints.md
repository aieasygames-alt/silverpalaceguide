## Keep the discovery in your hands

Silver Palace's announcement describes detective investigations alongside open-world exploration and action combat. For players who enjoy solving a mystery themselves, a complete answer can be more help than they wanted.

Our case-page format separates help into three choices. Each begins closed. Opening one does not automatically open the next.

## 01 — A gentle nudge

This level points your attention toward a question, detail or contradiction worth revisiting. Start here if you want to keep doing the reasoning yourself.

## 02 — A closer look

This level can identify relevant evidence, interactions or connections. It may reveal information you have not discovered yet, so open it only when a broad hint is not enough.

## 03 — The full explanation

This level is for the complete method and reasoning. It carries a separate spoiler confirmation. You can close everything again with the hide-all control.
