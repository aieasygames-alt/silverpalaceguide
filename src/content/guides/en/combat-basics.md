## Read this as an official beta reference

Elementa's **22 July 2026** combat article describes mechanics for that test. It is useful preparation, but final controls, timing windows and numerical effects may change. The flow diagram above is our summary of the announced mechanics, not a recorded combat test.

## Watch the warning, then parry

The article describes a **yellow circle** appearing when an enemy prepares an attack. A well-timed **Parry** just before impact reduces incoming damage and deals damage to the enemy. A successful parry can lead into follow-up combos.

The announcement does not establish a universal parry window or confirm that every attack can be parried. Learn the cue from the game tutorial for your platform instead of assuming a timing in milliseconds.

## Stun, execute, then use the opening

When an enemy is **Stunned**, an **Execution** can deal substantial damage. The article then describes the enemy entering a temporary **Weakened State**, creating an opportunity for further attacks.

This gives a useful sequence to recognize: stun → execution → temporary weakness. It does not supply an exact duration, damage multiplier or a guaranteed strategy for every enemy.

## Switching can preserve an outgoing action

In **Synchronised Combat**, switching during a **Charged Attack** or **Charged Skill** lets the outgoing character finish the action before leaving the field. This can briefly put multiple characters' actions on the battlefield together.

The sequence is: start a charged action → switch → let that action finish while using the next character. This explains the announced mechanism; it does not establish an optimal rotation, a team size or that all skills behave identically.

## Build resonance through attacks

The article says attacks build a character's **Resonance** meter. Filling it triggers an effect tied to their **Reactor** attribute and increases their damage. Complete attribute lists, stack rules and numerical values are not provided there.

Our [character dossiers](../../characters/) collect equipment and combat descriptions where the official introductions support them. Those descriptions alone cannot fill in each character's complete resonance kit.

## Casual Mode is an announced option

The beta article describes **Casual Mode** making combos easier through repeated left clicks. It also describes time slowing and a prompt before the character is hit. “Left click” is the article's PC example; it does not confirm final controller or touch bindings.

The official Japanese name is **シンプルモード**. Keep the localized name in mind when comparing settings with a player using another language.
