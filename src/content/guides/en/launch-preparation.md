## When is the Silver Palace release date?

Elementa's [announcement dated 31 October 2026](https://www.gamespress.com/Silver-Palace-Sets-Global-Launch-Date-for-December-14-2026) schedules the global launch of **Silver Palace for 14 December 2026 (UTC+8)**. The announcement does not specify an opening hour. A calendar date alone should not be treated as a midnight opening time.

Check the [official global website](https://silverpalace.elementagames.com/en-us/) for the latest opening time, preload instructions and any regional changes before arranging your first session.

## What time does Silver Palace launch in my timezone?

**An exact local opening time cannot be calculated from the cited announcement.** UTC+8 identifies the timezone attached to its release date; it does not supply the missing hour. Depending on the eventual opening time, your local calendar date could also differ.

Once an official opening hour is available, convert that complete date and time to your local timezone. The countdowns in our [event reward worksheet](../../tools/event-rewards/) are for the event's published draw and claim deadlines, not a game-launch countdown.

## Which platforms are announced for Silver Palace?

The announced platforms are **PC, PlayStation 5, iOS and Android**. The release announcement links to the Epic Games Store, PlayStation Store, App Store and Google Play. Store visibility and device compatibility can vary by region; consult the listing for your account and device.

Our [platform and download reference](../platforms-downloads/) collects the links supplied in that announcement. We do not host installers.

## Does Silver Palace support English and Japanese?

The announcement lists 12 languages: English, Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Indonesian, Thai, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese and Russian. This list does not establish voice-language coverage for every platform.

Silver Palace Guide begins with English and Japanese. Its language selector changes the guide website, not your game account or in-game language.

## A practical preparation list

- Follow the official announcement for your region and verify the opening time and timezone.
- Check your device against the requirements on its official store listing.
- Confirm account-linking and cross-platform rules from official documentation before making account decisions. This release announcement does not settle those details.
- Decide how much help you want with story puzzles. Our [case hint approach](../reading-case-hints/) lets you choose how far to read.
- Keep a note of questions you want answered after launch. Our exploration checklist stores your own notes in this browser.

## What remains unverified here

We have not established final launch-version material costs, drop rates, reroll recommendations or character rankings from this announcement. Those need separate evidence. Our current material planner uses requirements you enter yourself, rather than unverified character presets.

## Can I keep drawing web-event rewards until launch?

**The published draw deadline is 13 December 2026, 23:59 (UTC+8), before the announced launch date.** The official Mystery Unveiled event has a separate post-launch claim deadline of 29 December 2026, 10:00 (UTC+8). Drawing rewards and claiming them are different steps; do not wait until launch to finish your draws.

Read the [event guide](../mystery-unveiled/) for the rules, then use the [reward worksheet](../../tools/event-rewards/) to plan up to six drawn entries. Claiming happens manually on the official event site.
