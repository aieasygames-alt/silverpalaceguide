## Start with the official listing

The 31 October 2026 global release announcement names PC, PlayStation 5, iOS and Android. It supplies the following links:

- [Official website / PC information](https://silverpalace.elementagames.com/en-us/)
- [Epic Games Store](https://store.epicgames.com/p/silver-palace-9c18fb)
- [PlayStation Store](https://store.playstation.com/en-us/concept/10015692)
- [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6758292278)
- [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.elementagames.silverpalace.google)

The PlayStation link uses the US storefront supplied in the announcement. Choose the appropriate region in your store. An announcement link is not a guarantee of availability for every country or account.

## System requirements

We have not verified a complete launch-version PC specification or supported-device list for this guide. Use the requirements on the current official listing. Avoid treating a beta tester's hardware or a third-party estimate as an official minimum specification.

For mobile, check both the operating-system requirement and the store's device compatibility result. Storage requirements and preload sizes should be checked again close to release.

## Account and platform questions

The launch announcement does not establish every account-linking, cross-save, regional service or purchase-transfer rule. If you plan to play across devices, confirm those rules with official support before linking or purchasing.

We will update this reference when those details have a clear official source. Until then, the safest starting point is the official site and your device's own store listing.
