## When do Mystery Unveiled draws and reward claims end?

**Mystery Unveiled** is the official pre-launch web event linked from the game website. Its published draw period is **31 October 2026, 12:00 to 13 December 2026, 23:59 (UTC+8)**. In-game reward claiming opens after the game launches and ends on **29 December 2026 at 10:00 (UTC+8)**. The event rules do not give an exact claim-opening hour.

The interval between the end of draws and game launch is a preparation period: the rules say claiming is unavailable then. After launch, return to the official page to claim manually. The rewards are not automatically delivered just because you drew them.

Our [reward worksheet](../../tools/event-rewards/) displays both deadlines in your timezone and offers a calendar download. Check official updates before relying on a saved calendar entry.

## Two currencies, two pools

**Mind Lenses** are used for the **Clue Archives** in-game item pool. Each account can earn up to **10**. **Evidence Keys** are used for the **Core Evidence** physical-prize pool, with a maximum of **11** per account. They cannot be exchanged or substituted for one another. Unused draw currencies expire at the end of the event.

The task rewards are:

- Complete game pre-registration on the current account: one Mind Lens, once during the event.
- Complete and share the Detective Personality Quiz: one Mind Lens and one Evidence Key, once during the event.
- Share the event page: one Mind Lens per day. This task refreshes at 00:00 UTC+8; missed tasks do not carry over.
- Refer a friend who has not already pre-registered and have them complete registration through your referral link: one of each currency, up to ten qualifying referrals.

The overall currency caps still apply even when tasks remain available. Existing registrants, invalid referrals and other disqualifying situations listed in the rules do not count. The personality quiz is entertainment; its result does not establish a character's in-game abilities.

## What is in the ten-entry item pool?

Each Clue Archives draw costs one Mind Lens and removes one pool entry. There are ten entries in total:

- The Curiosity Mystery, described as a **4-star Motive**: ×1, one entry.
- Simulation Shot: ×1, one entry.
- The Science of All Things: Volume I: ×10, two separate entries.
- Paper Bookmark of Conspiracy: ×10, two separate entries.
- Hero's Instinct: ×10, two separate entries.
- Ounce: ×20,000, two separate entries.

Ten draws collect all ten entries. An item can appear under the same name twice because those are distinct entries. Selecting both Ounce entries, for example, uses two claim slots and totals 40,000 Ounces.

These quantities describe this event. They are separate from the global pre-registration milestone campaign. The rules do not establish item exchange values or complete equipment and upgrade systems.

## How do I claim my Mystery Unveiled rewards?

The Event Inventory stores the items you have drawn. You may select **up to six entries** from it. Our [reward worksheet](../../tools/event-rewards/) helps record your drawn entries and totals your planned selection. It does not access your game account.

Before the official claim:

1. Create a character in the game on your Silver Palace account.
2. Return to the official event page after claiming becomes available and open Event Inventory.
3. Select up to six drawn entries, checking separately listed copies.
4. Choose the character to receive them and review the final confirmation.

**The official claim is available only once. After confirmation, neither the selected items nor the recipient character can be changed.** Complete the manual claim before the published claim deadline. Some English rule wording refers broadly to the “event period”; the dedicated duration and claiming sections explicitly give the post-launch claim period above. Recheck the official Rules dialog for updates.

## Do duplicate rewards use more than one claim slot?

**Yes. The limit is six drawn entries, not six different item names.** Two Ounce entries occupy two slots and total 40,000 Ounces; that leaves room for up to four other entries. A quantity shown inside one entry, such as ×10, still belongs to that single entry.

In the [reward worksheet](../../tools/event-rewards/), mark the entries you actually drew before planning your selection. The worksheet totals quantities while keeping separately drawn copies distinct. It does not submit or reserve a claim.

## Which six rewards should I choose?

**The event rules do not establish one best selection for every player.** A comparison needs your drawn entries and the relevant items' confirmed uses. The rules alone do not supply complete upgrade costs or an exchange-value ranking, so this guide does not assign one.

Before the one-time official confirmation, check the account, recipient character, selected copies and quantities. Recording a selection in our worksheet does not claim it; complete the manual step on the official event site and review its confirmation.

## Physical prizes have separate rules

Core Evidence uses Evidence Keys and does not guarantee a win. Each account can win only one physical prize during the event. Winners must provide shipping information through the official event site by its stated deadline. Review the official eligibility, delivery and address rules there; the guide does not collect shipping details.

## Find the official rules

Open the [official event](https://silverpalace.elementagames.com/special/overture/), select **English** in its language menu and choose **Rules**. The same URL serves several languages. This guide independently summarizes the public rules checked on 22 November 2026; official changes take precedence.
