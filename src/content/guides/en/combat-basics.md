## Read this as an official beta reference

Elementa's [**22 July 2026** combat article](https://silverpalace.elementagames.com/en-us/newsDetail?id=93) describes mechanics for that test. It is useful preparation, but final controls, timing windows and numerical effects may change. The flow diagram above is our summary of the announced mechanics, not a recorded combat test.

## How do you parry in Silver Palace?

The article describes a **yellow circle** appearing when an enemy prepares an attack. A well-timed **Parry** just before impact reduces incoming damage and deals damage to the enemy. A successful parry can lead into follow-up combos.

The announcement does not establish a universal parry window or confirm that every attack can be parried. Learn the cue from the game tutorial for your platform instead of assuming a timing in milliseconds.

## Stun, execute, then use the opening

When an enemy is **Stunned**, an **Execution** can deal substantial damage. The article then describes the enemy entering a temporary **Weakened State**, creating an opportunity for further attacks.

This gives a useful sequence to recognize: stun → execution → temporary weakness. It does not supply an exact duration, damage multiplier or a guaranteed strategy for every enemy.

## Does every successful parry trigger an execution?

**The cited beta article does not establish that chain.** It describes a parry as a response to an incoming attack, and an execution as an action against an enemy that is already Stunned. It does not say that every successful parry causes Stun.

When comparing a gameplay clip with these descriptions, look separately for the attack warning, the enemy's Stunned state and the execution action. A short clip showing them close together does not establish a guaranteed sequence or a timing window. Check the current in-game tutorial before relying on it.

## What happens when you switch during a charged action?

In **Synchronised Combat**, switching during a **Charged Attack** or **Charged Skill** lets the outgoing character finish the action before leaving the field. This can briefly put multiple characters' actions on the battlefield together.

The sequence is: start a charged action → switch → let that action finish while using the next character. This explains the announced mechanism; it does not establish an optimal rotation, a team size or that all skills behave identically.

## Build resonance through attacks

The article says attacks build a character's **Resonance** meter. Filling it triggers an effect tied to their **Reactor** attribute and increases their damage. Complete attribute lists, stack rules and numerical values are not provided there.

Our [character dossiers](../../characters/) collect equipment and combat descriptions where the official introductions support them. Those descriptions alone cannot fill in each character's complete resonance kit.

## How are Reactor, Resonance and Motive related?

The checked sources use these terms in different contexts. They establish a link between **Reactor and Resonance**, but do not explain how **Motive** fits into a complete equipment system.

| Term | Confirmed description & source |
| --- | --- |
| Reactor | A character’s Reactor attribute is linked to their resonance effect. Alf’s introduction also describes a back-mounted Reactor that converts her shotgun into a flamethrower. **Source: July combat preview and official character introduction.** |
| Resonance | Attacks build the meter; filling it triggers a Reactor-related effect and increases damage. **Source: July combat preview.** |
| Motive | The Curiosity Mystery is described as a 4-star Motive in the web-event reward pool. **Source: Official event rules.** |

The **22 August feedback** also names **Catalysts / 触媒** alongside Reactors and buffs as effects that were difficult to notice during the test. It proposes clearer interface information and tutorial guidance. That notice does not define a Catalyst’s full rules or say it is the same object as Motive. Read the [development feedback explanation](../development-updates/) for that later plan.

The [Alf introduction](../../characters/#alf) gives a concrete use of a Reactor. It does not say that her weapon change is triggered by filling the resonance meter. Connecting those two descriptions into one activation rule would require further evidence.

Likewise, the [event rules](../mystery-unveiled/) confirm a reward’s name and type, rather than its equip slot, compatible characters, stats or relationship to a Reactor. A 4-star Motive reward is not a 4-star character reward. Keep that distinction when recording event entries or comparing character introductions; a recommended loadout needs additional equipment documentation.

## Does Silver Palace have an easier combat mode?

The beta article describes **Casual Mode** making combos easier through repeated left clicks. It also describes time slowing and a prompt before the character is hit. “Left click” is the article's PC example; it does not confirm final controller or touch bindings.

The official Japanese name is **シンプルモード**. Keep the localized name in mind when comparing settings with a player using another language.
