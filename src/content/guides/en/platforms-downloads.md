## Where can I download Silver Palace?

The 31 October 2026 global release announcement names PC, PlayStation 5, iOS and Android. It supplies the following links:

- [Official website / PC information](https://silverpalace.elementagames.com/en-us/)
- [Epic Games Store](https://store.epicgames.com/p/silver-palace-9c18fb)
- [PlayStation Store](https://store.playstation.com/en-us/concept/10015692)
- [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6758292278)
- [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.elementagames.silverpalace.google)

The PlayStation link uses the US storefront supplied in the announcement. Choose the appropriate region in your store. An announcement link is not a guarantee of availability for every country or account.

## Is a store listing the same as an available download?

**No. A listing or pre-registration link alone does not confirm that the game can be downloaded or played now.** Open the official listing for your account's region and check whether it currently offers registration, a preload or an install. A preload, if offered, is also separate from the game's server-opening time.

The cited release announcement does not establish a complete preload schedule or final download size for this guide. Check the [release date and launch-time reference](../launch-preparation/) before planning your first session.

## Is Silver Palace on Steam?

**This guide has not verified a Steam release.** The cited announcement links to the official PC website and Epic Games Store. Those links do not establish availability on another PC store, and the absence of a Steam link is not proof that a later release is impossible.

Use the official links above for the announced PC options and check new store announcements on the game's official website.

## What are the Silver Palace system requirements?

We have not verified a complete launch-version PC specification or supported-device list for this guide. Use the requirements on the current official listing. Avoid treating a beta tester's hardware or a third-party estimate as an official minimum specification.

For mobile, check both the operating-system requirement and the store's device compatibility result. Storage requirements and preload sizes should be checked again close to release.

Before downloading, compare the current listing with your device:

- **PC:** operating system, processor, graphics hardware, memory and available storage, wherever the official specification supplies them.
- **Mobile:** supported OS version, your specific device's compatibility result and available storage.
- **PS5:** the listing for your account's region and the storage information shown there.

These are checks to make, not a specification table. A missing requirement is unknown rather than a zero requirement.

## Does Silver Palace support cross-save or cross-play?

**The cited launch announcement does not confirm the full cross-save or cross-play rules.** A list of supported platforms alone does not establish either feature. Cross-save concerns using your progress across devices; cross-play concerns playing together across platforms. Neither term guarantees that purchases transfer.

If you plan to play across devices, confirm the supported account, region, progress-sharing and purchase-transfer rules with official support before linking or purchasing.

We will update this reference when those details have a clear official source. Until then, the safest starting point is the official site and your device's own store listing.
